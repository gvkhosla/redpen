# Decision: pilot shorter weekly planning

Approve a two-week pilot with one team: reduce the weekly planning meeting from
60 to 25 minutes, with written project updates shared before the meeting.

Use our existing shared document. Do not buy a new collaboration tool.

Reserve meeting time for blocked work, decisions, and owners. Keep background
status updates in the document.

Before starting, record current meeting time and decisions that get deferred.
During the pilot, track meeting time, unresolved decisions, and whether attendees
can identify their next actions. Review both time saved and decision quality before
recommending a wider rollout.

If approved, the head of operations will choose the pilot team and confirm an owner.
